2. What's Included

As a NeuroChiro Pro member, you receive:

3. Member Expectations

To get the most out of your membership, you agree to:

  • Complete your onboarding call with Dr. Ray within 14 days of signing up
  • Complete your directory profile (photo, bio, specialties, hours, contact info)
  • Respond to patient inquiries that come through the platform in a timely manner
  • Maintain active, ethical chiropractic practice

Members who do not complete the onboarding call within 14 days may have their account activation delayed until the call is completed.

4. Cancellation Policy
  • You may cancel your membership at any time by contacting support@neurochirodirectory.com or replying to any NeuroChiro email.
  • Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period. No partial refunds will be issued for the remaining days in a billing cycle.
  • Upon cancellation, your directory listing will be removed and dashboard access will be revoked.
  • Your profile data is saved. If you choose to rejoin in the future, your profile can be reactivated.

Founding member or promotional pricing is forfeited upon cancellation. Reactivation will be at the current membership rate.
